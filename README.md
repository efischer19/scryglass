Scrymat is the next chapter for the project that began as a local deck-utility app. The focus is shifting from a local, single-player experience toward a lightweight, shared virtual playmat for asynchronous, peer-to-peer card play.

---

## Current Direction

Scrymat is being reworked as a shared-state sandbox for tabletop card play. The platform will provide the table, the cards, and the zones, while players drive the rules through coordinated actions.
